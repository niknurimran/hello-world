## 1. Product Summary

### Product name and what it does
**Goldmap EA** is an Expert Advisor (automated trading program) for MetaTrader 4 and MetaTrader 5 that trades only **XAUUSD (gold)**. It runs inside the user's own MetaTrader terminal or VPS and handles entry, basket management and exit by itself, repeating the same rule-based cycle every day. The website's tagline is *"Gold trading on a fixed plan."*

### Main problem it solves
- Traders do not want to sit in front of the gold chart all day.
- Manual trading relies on prediction and guessing. Goldmap EA replaces that with *"the same disciplined cycle repeating."*
- Managing many separate positions is messy. Goldmap EA treats them as one basket (*"not 10 lonely trades fighting each other"*).

### Key features and benefits

| Feature (as stated on website) | Benefit |
|---|---|
| **Auto trade execution** | Set it once; the EA handles entry and exit, so there's no need to watch the chart constantly. |
| **Basket management** | All positions are treated as one basket and the EA manages total exposure, not one trade at a time. |
| **MT4 and MT5 ready** | Same logic, two builds. Use whichever terminal you're already comfortable with. |
| **VPS friendly** | Light on resources; a basic VPS is enough to keep it running 24/5. |
| **Daily target option** | Configurable daily target; once hit, the EA stops opening new cycles ("knows when to stop"). |
| **Built-in risk controls** | Lot cap, equity guard and spread filter are built into the settings. |
| **Tuned for XAUUSD** | Parameters shaped around gold behaviour, not a generic all-pairs template. |
| **Cent and standard accounts** | Start small on a cent account and scale later. Presets are provided for both. |
| **Scheduled entry pause** | New entries pause around rollover and thin-liquidity hours. |
| **Telegram support** | Support, updates and setting changes via the Telegram channel. |

**How it works (the 6-step cycle from the website):**
1. **Entry trigger:** the EA waits for its own structure condition on gold, then places the first position by itself.
2. **Basket management:** every open position belongs to one basket; the EA looks at total exposure.
3. **Recovery logic:** when price goes against the basket, built-in recovery rules adjust the plan.
4. **Structured exit:** take profit is calculated for the whole basket; once the target is reached, everything closes.
5. **Repeat the cycle:** basket closed, the EA resets and waits for the next valid condition.
6. **Scheduled pause:** around rollover and thin-liquidity hours, new entries are paused while existing positions are still handled.

**Spec sheet ("What you are actually running"):** Instrument XAUUSD · Platforms MT4 and MT5 · Order handling: basket, not single trades · Risk controls: lot cap, equity guard, spread filter · Daily target: configurable, then stops new cycles · Entry pause: rollover and thin-liquidity hours · Account types: cent and standard · Hosting: your own terminal or VPS · Build v4.2.3.

### Target customers
Based only on what the website says and implies:
- **Who:** Retail gold (XAUUSD) traders who already use, or are willing to use, MetaTrader 4/5 and a broker account. The footer says it is *"built for traders who prefer a routine over a hunch."*
- **Situation:** People who can't or don't want to sit in front of the chart all day; traders who want to start small (cent account) and scale later; people who want to run on a VPS 24/5.
- **Pain points:** Screen fatigue from watching charts, emotional or guess-based decisions, messy management of many separate trades, not knowing when to stop for the day.
- **Market/language:** The site mixes Malay phrases into its English, which suggests the core audience is **Malaysian** (inferred, not stated).
- **Age:** Not stated on the website (see Information to Verify).

### Pricing, packages or offers
- **Free access:** Funded by a broker partnership (you register with the partner broker). The website currently says **"Free access is closed for now."**
- **Paid:** *"After this closes, the cheapest way in is the 5 accounts licence at $299 USDT."* Paid licences *"work with any broker."*
- **Payment process:** The site does not take payments. Every paid option is an enquiry; the team replies on Telegram with payment details and licence steps.
- **Urgency mechanic:** A "Why now" section with a countdown and slot counter. The site states these are real: the countdown points to one real date, and the slot counter moves only when an actual application comes in.
- Other pricing tiers were not visible in the capture.

### Social proof available on the website
- **No testimonials, no profit screenshots, no performance chart, no member counts, on purpose.** The website explicitly says: *"We do not display member counts or account numbers we cannot prove to you."*
- What it offers instead: a **public Telegram channel** where updates, setting changes and users' own posts live (*"good days and bad days"*), plus a transparent **Target and Risk Calculator** that shows the downside next to the upside.
- **No partner or broker logos** are visible in the capture.

### Brand tone and style
- **Tone:** Casual, honest and anti-hype. Conversational Malaysian English with Malay phrases mixed in. Self-aware about risk ("no fortune telling", "no fake timers", "count both sides, not just the upside").
- **Visual style:** Dark navy/black background, bold white sans-serif headlines, red accent buttons and highlights, rounded cards and clean tables. Feels techy and premium-minimal.

### Main website calls-to-action
- **Primary:** **"Get Goldmap EA"** (red hero button and pricing section). Enquiries are completed on Telegram.
- **Secondary:** "See how it works", "Ask us" / "Ask Goldmap", "Open the Telegram channel", "Join us on Telegram", "Apply for free access" (currently closed), "Register with the broker", "Browse all questions", support@goldmapea.com.

## 4. Storyboards

### General production specs (apply to all 3 videos)
- **Aspect ratio:** 9:16 vertical, 1080×1920, 30 fps. Export a 1:1 (1080×1080) version for Facebook feed if needed.
- **Brand look:** Dark navy/black backgrounds, white bold sans-serif headlines, red accent (match the website's red "Get Goldmap EA" button). The website font appears to be Inter or similar (verify).
- **Captions:** Burned-in subtitles for all voiceover (most viewers watch muted). White text, dark semi-transparent box, safe zone kept clear of the platform UI (bottom 20% and right edge).
- **Persistent risk line:** In the final scene of every video, show the website's risk wording in small text: *"Trading gold with leverage can lose your entire capital. Not investment advice. No guaranteed returns. Only trade money you can genuinely afford to lose."*
- **No faces:** Hands-only B-roll, screen recordings and graphics only. Check stock clips for faces or reflections.
- **Do not show:** profit numbers, P/L screens, balance growth, "passive income" wording, or competitor branding.
- **Voiceover:** Relaxed, friendly, conversational English (a calm Malaysian English accent fits the brand, optional). Moderate pace, about 2.5 words per second.
