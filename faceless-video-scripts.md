> **Source:** All product facts come from the goldmapea.com homepage capture (24 Oct 2026, build v4.2.3). Anything not on the website is marked **[VERIFY]** and must be confirmed before recording.
>
> **Script rules applied to every video**
> - No faces. Visual cues describe screen recordings, graphics or hands-only B-roll.
> - No profit, income or "guaranteed" claims. No P/L screens or balance growth on screen.
> - Every video ends with the website's main CTA, **"Get Goldmap EA"**, and a risk line.
> - Voiceover pace: about 2.5 words per second for short-form, about 150 words per minute for long-form.

> **Standard risk line (end card, small text):**
> *Trading gold with leverage can lose your entire capital. Not investment advice. No guaranteed returns. Only trade money you can genuinely afford to lose.*

**How to read the scripts:** **VO** = voiceover (read exactly as written) · **TEXT** = on-screen text · **VISUAL** = short cue for the editor (full detail for Videos 1–3 is in the storyboard file).
